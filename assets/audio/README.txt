Put your own background music here, as mp3 files with these exact names:

  lobby.mp3      loops while students are joining
  question.mp3   loops while they answer a dish
  results.mp3    plays once on the final scoreboard

Any file you leave out is simply skipped — the game stays quiet in that phase.
Settings (volume, whether students hear it too) are at the top of assets/music.js.

Only use music you have the right to publish. This folder is served on a public
website, so anything in here can be downloaded by anyone with the link.
Royalty-free sources: Pixabay Music, Incompetech, Free Music Archive (CC tracks),
YouTube Audio Library.
